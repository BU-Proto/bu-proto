# Proto concept

Proto (working name) is a home in Boston for anyone who wants to build and make things: founders, engineers, CS people, designers, business students, and people with an idea and no team yet. Any school, or none. It started at BU and is run by BU students. It's free with no application, and meant to feel relaxed and social.

Proto isn't an accelerator and doesn't run a startup program. It's where people meet, find someone to build with, and get their work in front of others. Don't call it a "club" anywhere on the site.

## Who it's for

People who make things, or want to. Investors are where good work can lead, and firesides bring them in, but Proto isn't a place to collect VC contacts for a job or an internship. The copy and the events speak to people building something, and the fireside format keeps it that way (see firesides below). Business students are as welcome as engineers; what counts is wanting to make something.

## One community, with groups inside it

First comes one community on one calendar. Groups come after that, each with nights of its own:

- CS, the first group. Hackathons, hack nights, hackathon trips, the playbook, wins and the travel fund all live here: everything Proto did as a hackathon group.
- Business: pitch nights, finding customers, pricing and raising money, and teams for pitch competitions.

Every event is open to everyone. Some are for the whole community (build nights, firesides, demo night). A group's own nights say whose they are and that everyone else is welcome too, and that's how they're promoted: "CS group, everyone welcome".

On the site, each group has a page (/cs and /business) under Groups in the nav.

## Three ways in

The tracks are stages, not groups, and anyone in any group can be on any of them. Their names are stored with each member, so the names stay.

- Start: wants to make something and hasn't started. Any school, any major. Build nights with people who will help, and people with the skills they don't have yet.
- Build: making something, alone or with friends. Co-founders and teammates, feedback from people a step ahead, hackathon trips with the CS group.
- Launch: turning it into a company. Firesides with founders and investors, and a path into startup programs such as BU's Spark! and Innovate@BU.

## Rhythm

- Weekly: build night (was hack night). Open to anyone, with food. Bring what you're working on or come to meet people who are. Room and time not booked yet.
- Every two weeks (the goal): a fireside. A founder or investor talks about how companies get built, then takes questions from people building their own.
- About monthly: a group night, one group's own (CS: hack nights and hackathon trips; Business: pitch nights). Everyone else is welcome.
- Each semester: demo night. Anyone can show anything, finished or not.
- Later: our own hackathon (CS) and our own pitch competition (Business).

## Firesides

Two so far, both at BU and free with approval: Kent Bennett of Bessemer Venture Partners (Nov 17, 2026, with J and BU Cybersecurity Association) and Eric Silver, co-founder of Mass Magnetics (YC) (Nov 20, 2026, with Rionium). Each was a 45-minute conversation and 30 minutes of questions, with builders invited from BU, Wellesley, Tufts, BC and the rest of Boston.

To keep them builder-first (proposed, see docs/marketing-plan.md): the RSVP asks what you're building, seats go first to people with something to show, and investors come to see work, not to collect résumés.

## The fund (coming soon, not funded yet)

Planned microgrants of $50 to $500 for hackathon travel, registration, API credits or hardware, run with the CS group. The only ask is you demo what you built. We're still finding funding, so the site explains the plan and collects interest. The interest list is also how we show sponsors there's demand. No one can apply for money yet.

## Fits with BU and Boston

We're the community layer that feeds BU's programs (Spark!, Innovate@BU), not a competitor, and nothing is official with them yet. Proto is open to every school in Boston, and partners with groups at other schools on events, as the firesides already did.
