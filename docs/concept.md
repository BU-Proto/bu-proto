# BU Proto concept

BU Proto (working name). a BU-sponsored home base for anyone who makes things: first-time CS students, hackathon regulars, student founders. not framed as a club, don't use the word "club" anywhere on the site.

## three tracks

- Start: any CS student, no projects yet. beginner workshops, "first hackathon" squads paired with a veteran, project and resume help
- Build: hackathon regulars and side-project people. weekly hack nights, team matching, group trips (HackMIT, PennApps, etc)
- Launch: student founders. demo nights, founder talks, a path into BU's startup programs

## rhythm

weekly hack night open to everyone with food. group hackathon trips about monthly. one demo night each semester where anyone can show anything. year two: run our own BU hackathon.

## events

"why hackathons" night each semester (past winners show what they built and what it led to), hackathon prep nights the week before big events (ideas, team formation, pitch practice), panels with alumni/founders/recruiters, co-hosted workshops with sponsors and other BU orgs.

## the fund (COMING SOON, not funded yet)

planned microgrants of $50 to $500 for hackathon travel, registration, API credits or hardware. the only ask is you demo what you built. we're still finding funding, so the site explains the plan and collects interest. the interest list is also how we show sponsors there's demand. no one can apply for money yet.

## fits with BU

we're the community layer that feeds BU's existing programs (Spark!, Innovate@BU), not a competitor. nothing is official with them yet.
